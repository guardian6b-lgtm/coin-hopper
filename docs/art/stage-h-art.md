# Stage H local artwork and animation provenance

The official original PNG and generated Stage D atlas remain byte-for-byte unchanged. Stage H adds original Canvas2D drawings authored in `index.html`; no downloaded third-party artwork, fonts, CDN or runtime network request. The original `characters-v1.json` still describes the exact unmodified raster source. This is not a regenerated PNG or full 3D engine conversion.

The native gameplay renderer samples the atlas's richly shaded head/torso and ornaments, clips static extremities, and articulates separately shaded limbs. `RUN_CYCLE` contains eight contact/passing/flight frames tied to 64px traveled per stride. Back/front feet alternate planted and lifted states and arms counter-swing. Facing mirrors the whole articulated figure. Idle/run share torso registration, preventing a separate character style when locomotion begins. Jump/fall/hurt/victory retain the atlas poses, landing retains the original squash. Essential footsteps remain in reduced-motion; decorative bob/flash is suppressed. Simulation time/distance freezes animation during pause. The fallback vector poses remain available if image decoding fails.

Scenery adds broad receding mountain planes, round shaded trees, a terrain-anchored connected castle with keep/towers/walls/arched gate/bridge/flags/masonry, brick aqueduct, star observatory, moonlit pavilion, crystal galleries and summit ruins. All six retain their palettes. Fixed top-left lighting, beveled tile fronts, shaded forms and occlusion provide 2.5D presentation over the existing 2D simulation. Decorative architecture has no collisions; platforms keep bright consistent top edges, hazards keep high-contrast metal tips. The foreground tint is restricted to the lowest strip rather than covering hazards.

Shield art is a gold-rimmed faceted stone/jewel talisman instead of a checkmark. High Jump is a pair of leaf-laced boots. Active shield facets, leafy snare, layered flame/trail, lightning, star wings and crown impact share material-aware shading. Enemy locomotion uses discrete foot/wing frames, with hopper anticipation and armor/reaction states. Stone Guardian uses jointed articulated stone plates, recessed eyes, moss, seams and opening crown plates. Boulders/debris/ground stone waves replace generic glowing boss shots; warnings appear in-world with raised fists, gathering stone or marked ground.

These are implementation choices submitted for owner art acceptance, not a claim that procedural art matches every detail of the approved painted reference. Native before/after frames, all-hero strips and normal-play footage must be reviewed alongside the image. No atlas enlargement is presented as native-size evidence; diagnostic enlargements are explicitly labeled.
